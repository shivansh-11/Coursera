# Game Theory

### Recommended Readings

* The level of this course is similar to that of [1] (this book has a lot of interesting stories and examples). If you want to understand some of the technical concepts in more precise mathematical formulation, [2] or [3] would be wonderful references.


    1. Dixit and Nalebuff, The Art of Strategy: A Game Theorist's Guide to Success in Business and Life. W. W. Norton & Company (January 4, 2010)

    2. Gibbons, Game Theory for Applied Economists. Princeton University Press; Reprint edition (August 2, 1992)

    3. Tadelis, Game Theory: An Introduction. Princeton University Press (January 6, 2013)

    * Game theory create mathematical models to examine how people behave in strategic situations.
